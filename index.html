How to play
  1. Pick a tower from the cards at the bottom.
  2. Tap a grass tile to build. Drag to move the camera, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  3. Start the wave and stop the monsters before they reach the castle.
  4. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it. Kills earn gold.
Ballista: fast shots Cannon: splash damage Frost: slows enemies
